Structural roof assessment

Is your roof ready for solar?

We review how the additional panel and mounting loads affect your roof before installation. The assessment helps guide the layout and identify any work the roof may need.

  • Review of roof capacity, wind uplift and snow loads
  • Solar design, calculations and a clear proposal
  • Material take-offs and coordination with installers

Frequently asked questions

Can MKF handle both design and construction?

Yes. We can help with the design, structural assessment, planning and Building Regulations requirements, and construction.

Do you work on homes and commercial properties?

Yes. We work on residential projects such as extensions, conversions and renovations, as well as commercial fit-outs and solar enquiries.

What areas do you cover?

We take solar enquiries across the UK. For construction and design, our main service area is Greater London. Share your postcode so we can confirm availability.

How do I get a quote?

Send your postcode and a short project brief. We can discuss the scope, arrange a site visit where needed and provide a written quote.

What does the quote include?

The written quote sets out the agreed scope and pricing. We discuss changes to the scope with you before proceeding.
